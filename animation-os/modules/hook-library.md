# Hook Library

Stop the scroll in the first 2 to 3 seconds. The hook opens the curiosity loop the payoff closes.
Pick one, tuned to the product. Never answer the hook's question early.

## Templates

- **Curiosity gap:** "What happens if you ___?" · "What would happen if you ___ every day?" ·
  "How long can you ___ before ___?" · "How many ___ does it take to ___?"
- **Contradiction / correction:** "This is ___. And this is ___. Know the difference." ·
  "Everything you know about ___ is wrong."
- **"Stop doing X":** "Stop ___ every day." · "You might as well just ___."
- **Pattern interrupt:** open on a jarring, slightly weird, instantly identifiable visual (the
  reference for our test ads opened on a skeleton screaming in a jelly fat suit).
- **Visual before/after:** show the two states side by side before any words land.
- **Stakes / threat:** "You have ___ before ___." · a countdown or timer.

## Retention craft (weave in — see also `retention-devices.md`)

- **Open the loop in the hook, pay it off at the end.** Every beat makes the viewer more desperate
  for the answer.
- **Stack mini-loops between beats** ("…but that's nothing compared to Day 30").
- **Earn the product — the turn.** Build the tension first, then pivot on a single clean hinge line
  ("Then you find [product]"). The product is the answer to the loop, not an ad break.
- **Specificity = believability.** Concrete numbers, times, physical detail beat vague claims.
- **One idea per beat.** Cramming two kills rhythm and the loop.
- **CTA rides the momentum.** End on the transformation, then a soft, confident CTA tied to the offer.

State which loop you opened and where the turn to the product lands when you present the script.
