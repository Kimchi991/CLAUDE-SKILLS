# Style Pack — Bare-Bones Skeleton

**Best for:** origin/scenario, transformation, and cost-of-inaction ads. The proven house look and the
default cinematic Skeleton mode.
**Auto-recommend when:** no strong signal points to another style (this is the default).

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> A full anatomical skeleton with natural adult human proportions, tall and lanky, smooth ivory-cream
> bones with realistic bone detail (NOT toy-smooth, NOT chibi, NOT scary), and large expressive
> cartoon eyes with white sclera and dark pupils set in the eye sockets, giving an emotive, lovable
> face. No clothing. The same character in every shot. NOT realistic human, NOT Roblox, NOT Pixar.

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact cinematic 3D skeleton render of the attached `@CHARACTER`: photoreal [THEME]
> environment, warm [palette] grade, soft volumetric light, shallow depth of field. Do not restyle.

## World defaults
Photoreal cinematic environment dramatizing the ad's world (era, industry, fantasy); warm muted grade;
soft volumetric light with drifting atmosphere; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
Dry, subtle, restrained comedic performance — humor from situation and timing, not exaggerated acting.
Use eye direction and small head moves to sell each beat.

## Negatives (short)
realistic human, human in costume, horror skeleton, zombie, gore, Roblox, anime, Pixar, chibi, text,
style drift.

## Notes
- Eyes visible and readable in every shot.
- Great with the **World Engine** — drop the ordinary skeleton into a vivid era and escalate reactions.
