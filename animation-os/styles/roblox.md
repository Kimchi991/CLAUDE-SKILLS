# Style Pack — Roblox

**Best for:** playful, meme-native product ads; younger/social audiences; anything that benefits from a
relatable, funny blocky-avatar world.
**Auto-recommend when:** the brand is playful, the audience is social/meme-native, or the story wants a
lighthearted everyman character.

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> A friendly blocky Roblox-style 3D avatar: classic Roblox proportions, rectangular blocky torso,
> cylindrical blocky arms and legs, a boxy head; a FLAT PRINTED decal face (two large round black-pupil
> eyes and a small friendly mouth printed on the front of the head, NOT sculpted or extruded); short
> [color] molded Roblox hair as one solid piece (NOT realistic strands); [WARDROBE + COLORS]; GLOSSY
> HARD PLASTIC surfaces with crisp hard edges and clean specular highlights (like a shiny toy / game
> asset), rendered like a game-engine avatar (Roblox Studio look). The same character in every shot.
> NOT realistic, NOT a detailed human, NOT Pixar, NOT clay/matte.

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact Roblox art style, materials, proportions, face, and render of the attached
> `@CHARACTER`, rendered like a game-engine avatar (Roblox Studio look): GLOSSY hard plastic, crisp
> hard edges, flat printed decal face. No Pixar, cartoon, anime, skeleton, photorealistic, or
> claymation/clay/matte-sculpted look.

## The clay trap (the #1 Roblox drift — always guard it)
"Matte" surfaces and sculpted facial features make the model render **clay/claymation** instead of
Roblox. Always specify: **glossy hard plastic**, **crisp hard edges**, a **flat printed decal face**
(eyes/mouth printed, not sculpted), and a **game-engine render**. Never write "matte." Put the clay
terms in the negatives (below).

## World defaults
Clean, bright, colorful blocky game world; polished stylized geometry; soft cinematic lighting; keep it
premium game-cinematic, never a flat game-editor screenshot; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
Snappy but controlled, mild squash-and-stretch, simple gestures. Keep motion simple; the blocky look
reads best with clean, readable moves.

## Negatives (short)
claymation, clay, plasticine, Play-Doh, matte sculpted surface, stop-motion, sculpted 3D facial
features, dough-like material, soft rounded clay edges, fingerprints/handmade texture; realistic human,
detailed hair strands, Pixar, skeleton, anime, cartoon mascot, chibi, static mannequin pose, text,
watermark, style drift.

## Notes
- **IP caution:** use the Roblox-style *visual language* only. Never reproduce a real Roblox game, a
  named/existing Roblox character, or Roblox logos/UI.
- The biggest drift risk is flipping to a semi-realistic human — the render lock line above prevents it.
- Two sub-modes share this look: **Roblox Ad** (B-roll, VO carries) and **Roblox Story**
  (characters speak) — see `roblox-story.md`. Do not mix their rule sets.
