# Style Pack — Roblox

**Best for:** playful, meme-native product ads; younger/social audiences; anything that benefits from a
relatable, funny blocky-avatar world.
**Auto-recommend when:** the brand is playful, the audience is social/meme-native, or the story wants a
lighthearted everyman character.

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> A friendly blocky Roblox-style 3D avatar: classic Roblox proportions, rectangular blocky torso,
> cylindrical blocky arms and legs, a boxy head; a simple flat face with two large round eyes with
> black pupils and a small friendly mouth; short [color] molded Roblox hair as one solid piece (NOT
> realistic strands); [WARDROBE + COLORS]; smooth matte plastic surfaces, glossy premium game-cinematic
> 3D render. The same character in every shot. NOT realistic, NOT a detailed human, NOT Pixar.

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact Roblox art style, materials, proportions, face, and render of the attached
> `@CHARACTER`. Do not restyle. No Pixar, cartoon, anime, skeleton, or photorealistic look.

## World defaults
Clean, bright, colorful blocky game world; polished stylized geometry; soft cinematic lighting; keep it
premium game-cinematic, never a flat game-editor screenshot; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
Snappy but controlled, mild squash-and-stretch, simple gestures. Keep motion simple; the blocky look
reads best with clean, readable moves.

## Negatives (short)
realistic human, detailed hair strands, Pixar, skeleton, anime, cartoon mascot, chibi, static mannequin
pose, text, watermark, style drift.

## Notes
- **IP caution:** use the Roblox-style *visual language* only. Never reproduce a real Roblox game, a
  named/existing Roblox character, or Roblox logos/UI.
- The biggest drift risk is flipping to a semi-realistic human — the render lock line above prevents it.
- Two sub-modes share this look: **Roblox Ad** (B-roll, VO carries) and **Roblox Story**
  (characters speak) — see `roblox-story.md`. Do not mix their rule sets.
