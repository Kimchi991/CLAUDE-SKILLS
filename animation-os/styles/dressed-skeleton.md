# Style Pack — Dressed Skeleton

**Best for:** wearable, fashion, lifestyle, and identity-led products. The skeleton wears a full themed
outfit.
**Auto-recommend when:** the product is worn, or identity/lifestyle is the core benefit.

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> The same friendly skeleton (ivory bones, large expressive cartoon eyes with white sclera and dark
> pupils) wearing a complete [THEME-appropriate wardrobe, e.g. tailored suit / cowboy attire / varsity
> jacket]; skull, hands, and any exposed bones still visible. The same character in every shot. NOT
> realistic human, NOT Roblox, NOT Pixar. (Optional ghostly variant: semi-transparent clothing so the
> bones show through.)

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact cinematic 3D dressed-skeleton render of the attached `@CHARACTER`: photoreal [THEME]
> environment, warm [palette] grade, soft volumetric light, shallow depth of field. Do not restyle.
> Keep the exact wardrobe pieces and colors identical between shots.

## World defaults
Photoreal environment matching the product's world; warm grade; soft volumetric light; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
Wardrobe should read in motion (fabric sway, a coat catching light). Keep the outfit pieces identical
across shots — wardrobe drift is the main risk here.

## Negatives (short)
realistic human, human in costume, changed wardrobe, wrong outfit colors, Roblox, anime, Pixar, gore,
text, style drift.

## Notes
- Lock 2 to 3 outfit elements that must never change and restate them in the DNA every prompt.
- Eyes visible and readable in every shot.
