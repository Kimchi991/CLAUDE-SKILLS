# Style Pack — X-Ray Skeleton

**Best for:** health, body, biology, supplement, medical, and "what happens inside you" angles. The
only style that can literally *show* an internal mechanism (blood flow, follicles, organs, gut).
**Auto-recommend when:** the product's benefit is an internal/physiological process.

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> The same cartoon skeleton character with an x-ray-with-organs look: ivory bones under a faint
> translucent blue skin outline revealing glowing internal organs (heart, lungs, intestines in warm
> red/orange), and large expressive cartoon eyes with white sclera and dark pupils set in the sockets.
> [optional: wispy hair on the skull]. The same character in every shot. NOT realistic, NOT gory,
> NOT a horror skeleton, NOT Roblox, NOT Pixar.

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact x-ray-with-organs render of the attached `@CHARACTER`: cool translucent blue body,
> warm organ glow, clean sci-fi medical look. Do not restyle. No gore, no horror, no photorealism.

## World defaults
Clean medical/clinical space or a real setting with an x-ray glow overlay; cool blue base with warm
organ glow; soft rim light; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
The internal reveal is the spectacle: blood flooding vessels, follicles/organs reviving with a soft
pulse and particles. Pair with a **macro transition** (zoom from the outside into the x-ray).

## Negatives (short)
gore, blood splatter, open wound, horror skeleton, zombie, realistic human, Roblox, Pixar, text,
style drift.

## Notes
- **Eyes must stay visible and readable** in every shot (the character's whole expressiveness).
- Keep it clinical and clean, never grotesque — atmospheric internal visualization, not body horror.
- Internal x-ray inserts do not need the full body; they can be a macro cross-section in the same
  render language.
