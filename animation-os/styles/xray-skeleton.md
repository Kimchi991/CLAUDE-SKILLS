# Style Pack — X-Ray Skeleton

**Best for:** health, body, biology, supplement, medical, and "what happens inside you" angles. The
only style that can literally *show* an internal mechanism (blood flow, follicles, organs, gut).
**Auto-recommend when:** the product's benefit is an internal/physiological process.

## Style-lock summary
| Item | Master style lock |
|---|---|
| Style | Premium stylized 3D X-Ray skeleton animation |
| Character | Cartoon anatomical skeleton with expressive eyes |
| Body | Translucent blue anatomical body |
| Bones | Ivory-white, clean 3D bones |
| Organs | Visible through translucent body, subtle warm glow |
| Eyes | Large, expressive, fully rendered (never empty sockets) |
| Lighting | Cinematic, soft, polished, matches the environment |
| Tone | Funny, slightly absurd, visually punchy |
| Animation | Fast, readable, exaggerated but controlled — one action per shot |
| World | Realistic environments + stylized anatomy |
| Consistency | Every skeleton belongs to the same visual universe |

**Core identity in one line:** Premium cinematic 3D X-Ray animation featuring expressive blue
translucent anatomical skeleton characters with ivory-white bones, visible internally glowing organs,
large expressive eyes, believable environments, comedic physicality, fast-paced short-form advertising
movement, and one unified character universe across every shot.

## MASTER ANIMATION STYLE LOCK (attach to every shot)

```text
ANIMATION STYLE MASTER LOCK
Create a premium stylized 3D animated visual in a distinctive X-Ray with Organs aesthetic.
The entire animation belongs to ONE consistent visual universe.

CHARACTER DESIGN
Stylized 3D anatomical skeleton characters with expressive cartoon-like faces. The body is partially
translucent, revealing internal anatomy beneath the surface. Use: translucent blue anatomical body;
ivory-white skeletal structure; clearly defined bones; visible skull, ribs, spine, arms and hands;
visible internal organs with a subtle warm red/orange glow. Polished 3D animated characters, not
realistic medical anatomy. Anatomically inspired but intentionally stylized for advertising.

EYES
The eyes are a major part of the design: large, expressive, fully rendered, always clearly visible.
Communicate emotion through eye widening, side glances, focused contact, confused/surprised/playful/
confident looks, single-eye winks. Never use empty eye sockets as the default. Keep the eyes
consistent in shape, size, placement, and rendering.

FACE
Maintain recognizable skull and facial structure. Express through eyes, jaw position, mouth, head
tilt, body language. Comedic and exaggerated but visually coherent.

BODY AND ANATOMY
Believable 3D anatomy in a stylized commercial aesthetic. Smooth polished bone surfaces with subtle
variation. Translucent body reveals structures without becoming graphic. Organs subtly illuminated.
Use anatomy as a storytelling device; when the story calls for an internal view, transition naturally
into the anatomy rather than changing the whole visual style.

ENVIRONMENT
Place the stylized skeletons inside believable cinematic environments (bathroom, bedroom, street,
city, apartment, gym, store, restaurant, outdoor social, clinical/science). Grounded and detailed
environments with realistic materials and lighting; the contrast with the stylized characters is
intentional.

CHARACTER UNIVERSE
Every skeleton belongs to the same universe. Characters may differ in hair, clothing, build,
accessories, expression, personality — but ALL share the same translucent blue anatomy, ivory-white
bones, expressive eye design, organ visibility, rendering quality, material response, lighting
philosophy, and 3D character language. Never introduce a realistic human or an ordinary realistic
skeleton unless explicitly requested.

CAMERA LANGUAGE
Cinematic placement for short-form: straight-on, three-quarter, medium, medium-wide, high-angle
anatomical view, controlled product hero, mirror composition, dynamic tracking. Intentional, readable
movement: subtle push-ins, smooth tracking, controlled pans, small reframes, purposeful reveals.
Avoid random orbiting, extreme perspective, Dutch angles, and all-close-up shots. Leave room for
animation.

ANIMATION LANGUAGE
Fast, expressive, readable. Each shot communicates ONE primary action with clear anticipation, simple
action, clear reaction, strong payoff. Avoid multi-action sequences, unnecessary slow motion, random
gestures, constant movement. Every movement supports the beat.

COMEDIC PHYSICALITY
Visual comedy through exaggerated eye/facial reactions, head movements, hand gestures, body language,
timing, awkward pauses, quick reactions. Comedy comes from behavior and situation, not slapstick
chaos. Keep the character appealing and readable.

VISUAL STORYTELLING
Communicate the idea before the viewer processes the narration. Use strong visual metaphors (hair
roller → goofy reaction; bottle → dismissal; scalp → x-ray transition; blood vessels → follicle
activity; mirror → self-awareness; science environment → authority; product → solution; friends →
confidence; wink → playful CTA). Do not just stand and pose while the VO explains; the animation adds
what the VO does not show.

TRANSITIONS
Between normal views and x-ray anatomy, transition intentionally and connectedly: camera movement,
x-ray reveals, controlled anatomical cut-ins, match-like transitions, environmental continuity. No
random style switching, no flashy transitions unless the beat requires them.

LIGHTING
Premium cinematic, soft but directional, realistic environmental illumination. Skeletons receive the
same light as their surroundings. Subtle highlights on bones, translucent skin, eyes, hair, objects.
Organs may glow subtly. Do not make the whole character glow; avoid neon overload and flat lighting.

MATERIALS
Bones: smooth ivory-white, subtle realistic shading. Body: translucent blue, soft subsurface,
anatomical visibility. Eyes: glossy, expressive, clean reflections, large readable pupils/irises.
Hair: stylized but believable, individual strands where appropriate, consistent color/texture.
Environments: cinematic realistic materials, natural reflections, premium rendering.

COLOR LANGUAGE
Character: blue translucent anatomy, ivory-white bones, warm red/orange organs. Environments use
natural cinematic colors; do not force everything blue. The character is the visual anchor.

IMAGE QUALITY
Premium cinematic 3D: high detail, clean geometry, natural depth, controlled reflections, detailed
materials, clean edges, professional commercial animation. Avoid cheap stock 3D.

SHORT-FORM ADVERTISING LANGUAGE
Designed for TikTok/Reels/short-form. The first frame communicates the idea; actions read without
sound; visual changes happen often enough to hold attention; use comedic/surprising beats. Avoid long
static poses and generic corporate aesthetics. Feel internet-native and original.

PRODUCT INTEGRATION
When a real product is present, keep it visually consistent with its supplied reference. Animate the
character/environment around it. Do not redesign or invent packaging, or change the product's physical
identity. When exact product text matters, treat the supplied reference as the source of truth.
Product accuracy over unnecessary product movement. The product dominates the frame only on a
product-focused beat; otherwise integrate it naturally.

NEGATIVE STYLE LOCK
photorealistic humans; photorealistic or ordinary skeletons; realistic medical illustration; medical
textbook aesthetic; anime; manga; Pixar; Disney; Roblox; LEGO; claymation; flat 2D cartoon; comic
book; watercolor; plastic toy appearance; cheap CGI; low-poly; uncanny human; horror; gore; blood;
graphic anatomy; empty eye sockets; missing/closed/extra/deformed eyes; random character redesign;
style switching; inconsistent character rendering or anatomy; random camera angles; Dutch angle;
excessive motion blur or camera shake; slow motion; unnecessary particles; neon overload; overly
realistic skin; generic advertisement aesthetic.
```

## Locked rules (never break)
| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Same skeleton universe | Prevents style drift |
| Eyes always exist and stay consistent | The eyes are the character's personality |
| Blue translucent anatomy + ivory bones | Defines the visual identity |
| Organs visible | Makes the X-Ray concept recognizable |
| Real environments | Keeps the characters grounded |
| One action per shot | Makes image-to-video controllable |
| Fast visual storytelling | Prevents boring talking-head B-roll |
| Product only when useful | Stops repetitive product shots |
| Comedy through behavior | Makes the skeleton a character, not a prop |

## Notes
- This Master Lock is separate from the individual anchor prompt. Reuse it for any X-Ray skeleton ad,
  then attach the specific character, scene, product, action, camera, and duration underneath it.
- Internal x-ray inserts do not need the full body; a macro cross-section in the same render language
  is enough. Transition into them (do not hard-switch the style).
