# Style Pack — Anime

**Best for:** cooking, lifestyle, process, and stylized-story product ads; audiences who respond to
anime aesthetics.
**Auto-recommend when:** the product suits a stylized, expressive, process-driven or lifestyle story.

## Character Bible template (verbatim, every prompt)
> A stylized anime character: [age/build], [face + eye style — large expressive anime eyes, [color]],
> [HAIR — color, style, one key identifying detail], [WARDROBE + COLORS], clean anime cel/3D render.
> The same character in every shot. NOT realistic, NOT Roblox, NOT skeleton, NOT Western cartoon.

## Render / style lock line (every prompt)
> Match the exact anime art style, line quality, color treatment, and render of the attached
> `@CHARACTER`. Do not restyle. No photorealism, no Roblox, no skeleton, no Western cartoon.

## World defaults
Clean stylized anime environment fitting the story (kitchen, city, interior); soft cinematic anime
lighting; consistent palette; 9:16 vertical.

## Motion notes
Expressive but controlled; anime-style reactions and gestures. Keep the one identifying hair/wardrobe
detail visible in every shot (biggest drift risk).

## Negatives (short)
realistic human, photorealistic, Roblox, skeleton, Western cartoon, chibi (unless intended), extra
fingers, style drift, text, watermark.

## Notes
- Characters are still **B-roll** in Ad mode — the VO carries, no dialogue.
- Anime tends to drift on hair detail and eye style; pin them in the DNA and lean on the reference.
